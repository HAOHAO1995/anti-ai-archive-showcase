# Archive Structure / 档案结构

[Project overview / 项目首页](../README.md) · [Methodology / 研究方法](methodology.md)

This website is a **research archive, not a news feed**. Records are revised as new evidence, corrections and developments appear. A case can evolve for years; an event records a dated development within it.

网站是**研究档案，而非新闻信息流**。条目会随新证据、勘误和后续发展持续修订。案例可以跨越多年，事件则记录其中有日期的发展。

| Layer / 层次 | Purpose / 用途 | Reading route / 阅读入口 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chronology / 编年（时间线） | Dated developments since 2022; read in temporal order without assuming one final trajectory.<br>2022 年以来有日期的发展，保留时间顺序，不预设共同终点。 | [中文](https://salondesrefuses.cn/zh) · [EN](https://salondesrefuses.cn/en) |
| Cases / 案例 | Continuing disputes that link multiple events, actors and evidence.<br>串联多个事件、主体和证据的持续争议。 | [中文](https://salondesrefuses.cn/zh/cases) · [EN](https://salondesrefuses.cn/en/cases) |
| Documents / 文献 | Catalogue records of statements, policies, agreements, legal materials, works and related texts.<br>声明、政策、协议、法律材料、作品及相关文本的编目。 | [中文](https://salondesrefuses.cn/zh/documents) · [EN](https://salondesrefuses.cn/en/documents) |
| Sources / 来源 | Original URLs and source records supporting particular claims or entries.<br>支持具体主张或条目的原始网址与来源记录。 | [中文](https://salondesrefuses.cn/zh/sources) · [EN](https://salondesrefuses.cn/en/sources) |
| People & Places / 人物与地点 | Cross-reading through people, organisations and geographic categories.<br>通过人物、机构与地区分类开展交叉阅读。 | [中文](https://salondesrefuses.cn/zh/index) · [EN](https://salondesrefuses.cn/en/index) |
| Methodology / 方法 | Evidence, chronology, translation, verification and research limits.<br>证据、编年、翻译、核读与研究限制。 | [中文](https://salondesrefuses.cn/zh/about) · [EN](https://salondesrefuses.cn/en/about) |
| Art-History Timeline / 艺术史时间线 | A longer technical, intellectual and artistic context, with node-specific dates and sources.<br>提供较长时段的技术、思想与艺术背景，每个节点保留日期与来源。 | [中文](https://salondesrefuses.cn/zh/topics/ai-art-history) · [EN](https://salondesrefuses.cn/en/topics/ai-art-history) |

## Counts and relationships / 数量与关系

At the statistics sync of 28 September 2026, the reading room counted 516 document records and 2 authored essays together as 518 items. The event-linked people/organisation index showed 349 entries from 351 stored records, alongside 40 place categories. These are different counting scopes, not interchangeable totals.

2026 年 9 月 28 日统计同步时，阅览室将 516 条文献编目与 2 篇作者文章合计为 518 项；关联事件的人物与机构索引显示 351 条记录中的 349 项，另有 40 个地区分类。不同统计范围不可互换。

The 222 art-history nodes have 444 Chinese/English detail-page URLs; pages are not extra historical nodes. The three introductory guide cards have bilingual reading pages. See the [30 September reading update](../updates/2026-09.md) for the recorded navigation changes.

222 个艺术史节点对应 444 个中英文详情页网址；阅读页面不能重复计为历史节点。前三张导读卡片也有双语阅读页。已记录的阅读方式变化见[9 月 30 日阅读更新](../updates/2026-09.md)。

Repository research notes and historical statistics are indexed under [updates](../updates/README.md). The [data release policy](data-release-policy.md) distinguishes these public materials from the private internal dataset.

仓库研究札记和历史统计见[更新索引](../updates/README.md)。[数据发布政策](data-release-policy.md)区分这些公开材料与内部私有数据集。
