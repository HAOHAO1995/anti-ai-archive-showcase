# Research Questions / 研究问题

[Project overview / 项目首页](../README.md) · [Method / 方法](methodology.md)

## 1. Style and imitation / 风格与模仿

How are artistic styles identified, learned, reproduced or contested through generative systems?

艺术风格如何被识别、学习、复制、生成和争议？

## 2. Training data and scraping / 训练数据与抓取

What cultural materials enter training pipelines, and under what technical, legal or contractual conditions?

哪些文化材料进入训练流程？其技术、法律与合同条件是什么？

## 3. Consent and permission / 同意与许可

Who may permit or refuse training, generation, imitation, reuse or synthetic reproduction?

谁有权允许或拒绝训练、生成、模仿、再利用和合成复制？

## 4. Creative labour / 创意劳动

How do generative systems change the working conditions and bargaining relations of cultural workers?

生成式系统如何改变插画师、作家、演员、音乐人、配音演员、设计师、摄影师等文化工作者的劳动条件与协商关系？

## 5. Voice, likeness and digital replicas / 声音、肖像与数字替身

How are identity, embodiment, voice, face, performance and synthetic substitution negotiated?

身份、身体、声音、脸部、表演和合成替代如何进入新的权利谈判？

## 6. Copyright and licensing / 版权与许可

How do lawsuits, collective agreements, licences, settlements and revenue-sharing arrangements reshape generative markets?

诉讼、集体协议、许可、和解和收益分配安排如何重新塑造生成式 AI 市场？

## 7. Platforms and infrastructure / 平台与基础设施

How do platform rules, metadata, crawler controls, content labels, recommendation systems and defaults govern cultural information?

平台规则、元数据、爬虫控制、内容标签、推荐系统和默认设置如何治理文化信息？

## 8. Model and corporate governance / 模型与企业治理

How do AI companies, rights holders, cultural industries, governments and institutions negotiate access, restrictions, safeguards and market power?

AI 公司、权利方、文化产业、政府和机构如何协商模型访问、限制条件、技术保障与市场权力？

## From “Is it AI?” to “Under what conditions?” / 从“是不是 AI”到“在什么条件下”

From **“Is this AI?”** toward **“Under what conditions is this transformation considered legitimate?”** The question concerns the renegotiation of cultural information’s uses, not a single verdict on whether AI is good or bad.

从**“这是不是 AI？”**转向**“在什么条件下，这种转换被认为是合法、正当或可接受的？”** 问题在于文化信息使用条件如何被重新谈判，而不是为 AI 的好坏作出单一裁决。

## Analytical Distinctions / 分析对

| Analytical distinction / 分析对 | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| Authorised / Unauthorised | 授权 / 未授权 |
| Disclosed / Undisclosed | 披露 / 未披露 |
| Compensated / Uncompensated | 补偿 / 未补偿 |
| Traceable / Opaque | 可追踪 / 不透明 |
| Negotiated / Imposed | 协商 / 强加 |
| Opt-in / Opt-out | 主动加入 / 默认使用后可退出 |

Opt-in requires permission before participation or use; opt-out provides a way to refuse a default inclusion or use. The practical scope and effect must be checked in each case. / Opt-in 指使用或参与前主动许可；opt-out 指默认纳入或使用后可选择退出，而非默认不参加。应逐案核对其适用范围和实际效果。

Legal validity, social legitimacy and acceptability to affected parties are not interchangeable. These distinctions guide inquiry; they do not assign a case a definitive status. / 法律有效性、社会正当性和当事方可接受性不能混同。这些区分用于提出问题，不自动给案例定性。
