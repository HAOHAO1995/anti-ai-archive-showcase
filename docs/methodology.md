# Methodology / 研究方法

## Evidence and chronology / 证据与时间过程

- **Primary sources where available.** Prioritise artist statements, union documents, platform announcements, legal filings, corporate policies, public agreements and institutional notices.
- **Chronology over retrospective simplification.** Preserve changes in positions, claims and negotiations; do not reduce a case to its final outcome.
- **Multiple positions.** Artists, workers, unions, platforms, companies, governments and rights holders do not share a single position on AI.
- **Claims are not mechanisms.** Record statements, opt-out labels, crawler blocks and contracts as distinct forms of evidence; declared intention does not establish demonstrated technical effect.
- **Ongoing, non-exhaustive research.** Revise accounts as evidence changes; gaps do not prove that an event did not occur.

中文：

- **有条件时优先一手来源。** 包括艺术家声明、工会文件、平台公告、法律文件、企业政策、公开协议和机构通知。
- **保留时间过程，避免事后简化。** 保留立场、主张和协商的变化，不把案例压缩为最终结果。
- **保留不同立场。** 不假定艺术家、劳动者、工会、平台、企业、政府和权利方拥有统一立场。
- **声明、主张与实际机制不是一回事。** 分别记录声明、退出标签、爬虫限制和合同；公开承诺不自动证明技术效果。
- **持续修订且不求穷尽。** 随新证据更新叙述；未收录不代表未发生。

## Cases, events and status / 案例、事件与状态

A **case** is a continuing dispute or historical object. An **event** is a dated development within it. A document is a catalogued work or text; a source is evidence linked to a record or claim. Preserve date precision: a known year or month must not be turned into an invented day.

**案例**是持续发展的争议或历史对象；**事件**是其中有日期的发展。文献是被编目的作品或文本，来源是关联记录或主张的证据。保留日期精度，不把只有年或月的材料补成虚构的具体日期。

An allegation, announcement, filing, ruling, settlement and implementation describe different states. Attribute statements to the relevant party and date; a settlement is not automatically a ruling, nor an announcement evidence of implementation.

指控、宣布、立案／提交诉状、裁判、和解和实施属于不同状态。保留陈述主体与日期；和解不自动等于判决，公告也不自动证明实施。

## Languages, source review and rights / 语言、核读与权利

The website provides Chinese and English reading routes while retaining original-language titles, credits and URLs. Source review and translation review are recorded separately. **“Source-checked” does not mean every allegation has been independently verified.** Some translations may be AI-assisted and may not have received independent human review. Catalogue entries do not imply preservation or redistribution of third-party full texts; rights remain with their respective holders.

网站提供中英文阅读入口，并保留原语言标题、署名和网址。来源核读与翻译审校分别记录。**“来源已核读”不等于每项指控都已独立核实。** 部分译文可能含 AI 辅助，且尚未经独立人工审校。目录收录不表示保存或重新分发第三方全文；权利仍归各自权利人。

Archive translations must be identified as translations; translated summaries are not full translations. Do not invent publisher-provided language versions or imply that an external page has been preserved locally.

档案译文须注明翻译身份；译介摘要不等于全文翻译。不虚构出版者提供的语言版本，不将外部链接表述为已在本地保存的副本。

## Interpretation and correction / 解释与勘误

The patterns “Style → Data → Consent → Labour → Infrastructure → Control” and “Symbolic refusal → Contractual refusal → Technical refusal → Infrastructural refusal” are provisional research interpretations. They may overlap, change or be rejected; they are not archive facts or universal historical stages.

“风格 → 数据 → 同意 → 劳动 → 基础设施 → 控制”与“象征性拒绝 → 合同性拒绝 → 技术性拒绝 → 基础设施性拒绝”属于暂定研究解释，可能重叠、修订或被推翻，不是档案事实或普遍历史阶段。

For corrections, identify the page or stable record ID, the disputed wording, the proposed correction and its original evidence. Source availability, uneven geographic coverage and translation status constrain interpretation. See the [contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

勘误请提供页面或稳定编号、现有表述、拟议修改与原始证据。来源可得性、地区覆盖不均和译审状态构成解释的限制。参见[参与指南](../CONTRIBUTING.md)。
