## Reading cautions / 阅读提示

- **ArtStation / NoAI:** distinguish symbolic refusal, labels, platform defaults and demonstrable crawler controls. / 区分象征性拒绝、标签、平台默认设置与可证实的爬虫控制。
- **mimic:** distinguish upload eligibility, consent and platform responsibility; retain Japanese originals alongside archive translations. / 区分上传资格、同意与平台责任；对照档案译文保留日文原始材料。
- **SAG-AFTRA:** the linked case concerns the 2023 film and television strike. Distinguish tentative agreements, ratification and implementation. / 链接案例为 2023 年影视罢工；区分暂定协议、批准与实施。
- **Suno / Udio:** these are separate cases. A lawsuit, negotiation, settlement and implemented licence must not be conflated; revenue-sharing arrangements must be read at their recorded status and date. / 两者属于独立案例；诉讼、谈判、和解与已实施许可不能混同，收益分配安排须按记录的状态与日期阅读。
- **Seedance / film-industry disputes:** distinguish claims by rights holders, corporate announcements and demonstrated safeguards. / 区分权利方主张、企业公告和已证实的保障措施。
