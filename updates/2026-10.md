## 1 October: illustrated reading rooms / 10 月 1 日：图文阅读室

The four research entrances and Documents now use a consistent reading-room layout with record-specific English descriptions, source illustrations and separate reading pages. Duplicate research-guide headings and the central reading-room scrollbar/divider have been removed; scrolling remains available.

The three AI Art History introductions have relevant historical illustrations. Documents has illustrations for 320 of 518 entries; 198 entries still await suitable, reviewed images. One event card in the Anti-AI events topic also remains without an accepted image. Existing historical-node illustrations are a separate collection. No generic generated image is used to fill a gap.

## 1 October: mobile reading and author profile / 10 月 1 日：移动阅读与作者介绍

On narrow screens, navigation now stays in one horizontally scrollable row. Mobile reading rooms retain catalogue position when returning from an entry and preserve the selected preview when changing language. A tap on an AI art-history timeline node dismisses the mobile timeline panel. Index tabs and touch controls have been adjusted for narrow layouts. The About page ends with a centred LUOCAO author profile and the original red puzzle logo, with separate Chinese and English copy.
