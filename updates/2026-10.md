# October 2026 website updates / 2026 年 10 月网站更新

## 1 October: illustrated reading rooms / 10 月 1 日：图文阅读室

The four research entrances and Documents now use a consistent reading-room layout with record-specific English descriptions, source illustrations and separate reading pages. Cases, People & Places and Sources are available through the tabs in [Index](https://salondesrefuses.cn/en/index). About is the last navigation item, separated by a vertical rule. Duplicate research-guide headings and the central reading-room scrollbar/divider have been removed; scrolling remains available.

四个研究入口与文献页使用统一阅读室排版，提供逐条对应的英文描述、来源插图和独立阅读页。案例、人物与地区、来源可在 [Index 综合检索](https://salondesrefuses.cn/zh/index)中切换。About 位于导航末尾并以竖线隔开，研究专题页重复的标题及阅读室中间的滚动条、分隔线已移除，正常滚动仍保留。

The three AI Art History introductions have relevant historical illustrations. The research-guide and document illustration collection contains 316 distinct assets, including original images, document pages and clearly labelled present-day source-page excerpts. Documents has illustrations for 320 of 518 entries; 198 entries still await suitable, reviewed images. One event card in the Anti-AI events topic also remains without an accepted image. Existing historical-node illustrations are a separate collection. No generic generated image is used to fill a gap.

AI Art History 的三张导读卡片已配上相关史料图。研究导读与文献插图集合包含 316 份不同素材，包括原始图片、文件页以及明确标注采集日期的当前来源页面摘录。文献页 518 项中，320 项已配图，198 项仍待补充合适且核读过的图片；反 AI 事件专题另有一张事件卡片待配图。既有艺术史节点插图为另一集合，缺图处不使用通用生成图填充。

Current source-page captures document what the source page displayed on 1 October 2026; they are not historical screenshots of the event date. Captions retain provenance and distinguish archival translations from publisher text. Translation review and factual reading remain separate; independent human review is not claimed.

当前来源页面摘录记录 2026 年 10 月 1 日所见页面，不冒充事件发生时的历史截图。图注保留出处并区分档案译文与出版方原文。翻译审校与事实核读分别记录，不声称已经独立人工审校。

## 1 October: mobile reading and author profile / 10 月 1 日：移动阅读与作者介绍

On narrow screens, navigation now stays in one horizontally scrollable row. Mobile reading rooms retain catalogue position when returning from an entry and preserve the selected preview when changing language. A tap on an AI art-history timeline node dismisses the mobile timeline panel. Index tabs and touch controls have been adjusted for narrow layouts. The About page ends with a centred LUOCAO author profile and the original red puzzle logo, with separate Chinese and English copy.

窄屏导航整理为一条可横向滑动的目录。手机阅读室从介绍返回目录时保留浏览位置，切换语言时保留所选介绍；点击 AI 艺术史时间线节点后收起手机浮层。Index 切换按钮及触摸控件调整为适合窄屏的布局。About 末尾加入居中的落草作者介绍和原始红色拼图 logo，分别提供中英文文案。

This is an interface and author-profile update; the collection totals and research snapshot above are unchanged.

此次为界面与作者介绍更新，上述编目统计和研究快照不变。
