## 中文 · 9 月 20 日原札记

这是公开展示仓库的首期札记，以 2026 年 9 月 20 日已上线的网站为基线，不将现有全部记录计为本月新增研究。

公开版本包含 221 个案例、373 条事件、435 条文献记录和 444 条来源记录。数量反映编目规模，不代表每项主张都已独立核实，也不代表第三方全文已被本档案保存。

本次展示更新加入中英文网站截图、三个案例入口及网站的 GitHub 收藏入口。建议从三个问题开始：

1. [ArtStation / NoAI](https://salondesrefuses.cn/zh/cases/case-0001)：拒绝如何成为平台标签、筛选和访问规则？标签不能单独证明防抓取效果。
2. [mimic](https://salondesrefuses.cn/zh/cases/case-0003)：上传资格与同意如何影响平台的停服和后续重启？请同时阅读案例中的日文原始来源。
3. [SAG-AFTRA 2023 影视罢工](https://salondesrefuses.cn/zh/cases/case-0024)：数字替身的同意和报酬如何进入协商？需区分暂定协议、批准与暂停罢工。

后续工作是持续补充证据与修正叙述，而非预设这些案例导向同一结论。网站提供中英文阅读入口，保留原语言来源。翻译审校和来源核读独立记录；部分 AI 辅助译文尚待独立人工审校。艺术谱系部分仍在研究中，不能视为全部核实的历史。

欢迎通过 [Issues](https://github.com/HAOHAO1995/anti-ai-archive-showcase/issues) 提交可公开访问的来源和具体勘误。如果档案对你的研究有帮助，欢迎 Star 收藏。

## English · original note of 20 September

This is the public showcase repository’s first research note. Its baseline is the website published on 20 September 2026; existing records are not presented as research newly added this month.

The public edition contains 221 cases, 373 events, 435 document records and 444 source records. These figures describe catalogue size. They do not establish independent verification of every claim or local preservation of third-party full texts.

This showcase update adds Chinese and English website screenshots, three case entry points and GitHub star links on the website. Start with three questions:

1. [ArtStation / NoAI](https://salondesrefuses.cn/en/cases/case-0001): how does refusal become platform tags, filters and access rules? Tags alone do not establish anti-scraping effectiveness.
2. [mimic](https://salondesrefuses.cn/en/cases/case-0003): how do upload eligibility and consent inform suspension and later relaunch? Read the original Japanese sources linked in the case alongside the archive’s account.
3. [SAG-AFTRA’s 2023 film and television strike](https://salondesrefuses.cn/en/cases/case-0024): how do consent and compensation for digital replicas enter negotiations? Distinguish a tentative agreement, ratification and the suspension of a strike.

Further work will develop the evidence and revise accounts, without assuming that these cases lead to a shared conclusion. The website offers Chinese and English reading routes with original-language sources. Translation review and source checking are recorded separately; some AI-assisted translations await independent human review. The art genealogy remains in progress and should not be treated as a fully verified history.

Publicly accessible sources and specific corrections are welcome through [Issues](https://github.com/HAOHAO1995/anti-ai-archive-showcase/issues). If the archive supports your research, star the repository to find it again.
